# Shared structure-series CV redesign

## Implemented design

Retain UMAP as a reference and add shared five-fold Butina series splits on
Morgan radius-2/2048-bit fingerprints. Before clustering, collapse compounds to
InChIKey connectivity blocks; all stereoisomers therefore share a fold. Cluster
only 6,146 non-test compounds. Sort groups by descending size and allocate each
to the least populated fold. The resulting molecule/fold map is common across
all four regression targets, including auxiliary-only compounds.

Threshold 0.50 is the initial split. Because the selected hit-neighbor evaluation
subset was small, a pre-score sensitivity comparison uses threshold 0.40. Butina
clusters are center-based neighborhoods, not connected components or a guarantee
that every cross-fold similarity lies below the threshold.

Within each structure partition, identify the 25 highest-direct-pIC50 distinct
groups per CYP1A2, CYP2C9 and CYP3A4. Their union defines the hit-series subset;
CYP2D6 is evaluated on available labels in that same union. Evaluate three views:
all labeled compounds, hit-series compounds, and hit-series compounds excluding
all selected seed molecules. Fold construction is structure-only; the seed-based
views are explicitly outcome-selected diagnostics. Seeds are held out together
with their groups, so this is series-held-out evaluation, not the alternative
protocol where the parent hit remains in training.

## Matched models

Fit LightGBM L1 (300 trees, 15 leaves, learning rate 0.05, min child samples 20,
L2=1) and TabPFN 2.6 (eight estimators, mean prediction) on Mordred descriptors.
Median imputation is fitted independently on each endpoint's outer training fold;
all-null training columns are dropped by the imputer. Seeds are 42+fold. No
supervised log2fc features or old-fold preprocessing are reused. The threshold
0.40 comparison reuses identical UMAP OOF predictions; its new series models
are trained afresh. These are controlled baseline comparisons, not the previous
PCA/log2fc/Mordred ensemble.

No model settings were tuned to these scores.

The macro difference is approximately zero; the endpoint comparisons favor

subset remains potency-enriched; selecting other-CYP neighborhoods did not make
its observed labels predominantly weak. These distributions are part of the
readout rather than a reason to discard the experiment.

## Operational decision

Keep a **common fold map**, with endpoint-specific labels and readouts. Retain
UMAP as the broad generalization reference; adopt series threshold 0.40 as the
additional series-held-out evaluation because it offers more non-seed coverage.
Use its all-label and non-seed hit-neighbor results together. Keep threshold 0.50
as a sensitivity reference. Do not select threshold by its aggregate error.

For the next full feature-pipeline comparison, use the saved threshold-0.40 map
and refit all supervised auxiliary features under that map. Existing UMAP-based
auxiliary predictions must not be moved into the new folds. The fixed Mordred
baselines here already supply the first complete learner comparison.

## Score decomposition prompted by unexpectedly high endpoint values

Audit `20260911T104152754128Z-3c078c1e` independently rejoins OOF to original
labels by compound ID and endpoint; all labels match. For TabPFN on series0.40:

| Endpoint | All-label MAE | Neighbor MAE | All-label ST-RAE | Neighbor ST-RAE | Neighbor bias |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1A2 | .6541 | .7117 | .8212 | .9183 | -.1288 |
| 2C9 | .4660 | .4963 | .6632 | 1.0547 | -.1587 |
| 2D6 | .5974 | .6016 | .9224 | 1.1479 | -.3419 |
| 3A4 | .5622 | .5695 | .5594 | .6800 | -.1288 |

ST-RAE uses the mean label of the evaluated subset for its denominator. The
neighbor mean interval errors / denominators are .5129/.5585, .2946/.2794,
.4492/.3913 and .3641/.5355. Thus the high relative scores are not corresponding
jumps in ordinary MAE. Narrower experimental intervals on these subsets also
increase interval error; 2D6 mean CI width falls from .4922 to .3894.

Reporting correction: the neighbor-only table should have been accompanied by
all-label metrics and the fact that these are Mordred-only baselines. It is not a
comparison of the prior .69 full-feature ensemble under a new split. Moreover,
holding the seed out with its group measures new-series extrapolation, whereas
actual challenge acquisition expands known hits. A parent-retained, neighbor-held
protocol is a distinct and more direct test of that acquisition scenario; the
present protocol should be named series-held-out rather than test-reproducing.
