They are not five new independent assays each time redistributed through ChEMBL or EPA; deduplicate imported copies by original assay identity.

Raw acquisition `20260912T050912446287Z-84d9f97e` saves all five full CSVs and source descriptions. They include original deposited SMILES, SID/CID, overall activity outcome, three replicates, phenotype, potency, Fit_LogAC50, curve class, R2, QC notes and concentration response values. No inactive compounds were discarded during acquisition. The old NIH library ZIP URL returned404 and is recorded; it was unnecessary because PubChem includes deposited structures.

Inventory `20260912T051049331831Z-b9ec2232` saves `tox21-raw.parquet` and `tox21-replicates.parquet`, preserving original fields. The count of9,667 refers to SIDs, not unique standardized molecular structures.

Curation `20260912T051221903183Z-6113ed82` produces7,247 standardized compounds. After excluding all319 challenge connectivity overlaps,6,928 remain. Of these5,974 are absent from the *previous* combined external standardization inventory. This is not a count relative to the concurrently expanded ChEMBL dataset; the combined inventory computes that separately.

Usable files under that run's `outputs/`:

- `tox21-compounds.parquet`: compound_id, standardized_smiles, connectivity.
- `tox21-targets.parquet`: compound_id, isoform, aid, value, n_sids, label_kind, source.
- `tox21-curated-records.parquet`: all source rows, identities and overlap annotations, including challenge overlaps for later fold-safe calibration studies.
- `tox21-curated-replicates.parquet`: all replicate records with identities and numeric pAC50/R2/curveclass, without overlap filtering.

Binary labels use overall Active=1 and Inactive=0; Inconclusive is missing. Duplicate structures are averaged, yielding soft targets when different SIDs disagree. Potency seed labels require overall Active, replicate Inhibitor and curveclass -1.1,-1.2,-2.1,-2.2; median across eligible replicates. Source description defines Fit_LogAC50 using molar units, hence pAC50=-Fit_LogAC50. It is an assay-specific auxiliary label, not challenge pIC50. All other values remain available for alternative quality and ranking policies.

QC audit `20260912T051626523150Z-f516bbc3` found binary conflicts in166/327/90/196/74 compound labels for1A2/2C19/2C9/2D6/3A4. Overall Active records overwhelmingly have Inhibitor replicates; occasional opposing replicate phenotypes remain documented. QC provider text does not constitute a verified purity threshold, and no such purity claim is made.

## NVS_ADME: primary release recovered, structure linkage in progress

[EPA's current data page](https://www.epa.gov/comptox-tools/exploring-toxcast-data) identifies invitrodb4.3, August2025. Its Clowder metadata was readable, but repeated blob requests for the latest summary files timed out. Recorded attempts are `20260912T051117107354Z-a0439948` and `20260912T051402096067Z-add93607`.

The official EPA FTP still serves invitrodb3.3. Targeted HTTP range extraction avoided downloading the whole1.5GB ZIP: `20260912T051516725408Z-2f71d311` saves the original NVS CSV, assay annotations/methods and quality statistics. This is explicitly an older2020 release, not equivalent to latest4.3 reprocessing. EPA recommends latest releases for new analyses; retain this as a provisional external experiment only until refreshed.

Inventory `20260912T051719246898Z-6e74f1c5` selects five human CYP endpoints and preserves fit parameters, hitcalls, flags, concentration units and identities in `nvs-raw.parquet`. An initial numeric CSV inference failed on a fractional nrep; `20260912T051704681659Z-edf56361` is retained and recovery uses a raw string schema.

Do not infer pAC50 blindly: some NVS measurements have mg/l rather than uM units, control rows have hitc=-1, and inactive rows lack meaningful fitted potency. Molecular structures must be joined from a traceable identifier source before model use.

### NVS structure linkage completed

The original EPA DSSTox20181017 ZIP was recovered from the official FTP in `20260912T052007967609Z-73db76da`; extraction `20260912T052022908007Z-b3613d3a` retains the SDF and XLS. The SDF includes CASRN, DTXSID, deposited structure SMILES, InChIKey and substance notes.

Curation `20260912T052128343558Z-128af2eb` matched632 of656 CAS identifiers from1,432 raw NVS rows by exact CAS to the official DSSTox archive. No CAS mapped to multiple standardized structures. After removing36 challenge-overlap compounds and selecting representative samples (`gsid_rep=1`) with binary `hitc`0/1 and uM units,590 compounds remain. This produces1,655 source-specific auxiliary labels:

`nvs-compounds.parquet`, `nvs-targets.parquet` and complete `nvs-curated-records.parquet` are in that run's outputs. Target keys include compound_id, isoform, aeid and label_kind, with source=`nvs_adme_v33`. The older EPA model parameter `modl_ga` is log10 AC50 in the tested concentration units ([official tcpl documentation](https://gaftp.epa.gov/comptox/High_Throughput_Screening_Data/InVitroDB_V3.1/MySQL_Data/tcpl_v2_vignette.html)); for retained uM rows, pAC50=6-modl_ga. No inactive pseudo-potencies or mg/l-to-molar conversions are inserted. Potency seed targets additionally require hitc1, finite fitted potency and no curve flags.

This small multiconcentration subset has a high active fraction. It must not be presented as an unbiased population activity prevalence or as all976 chemicals from the earlier paper. Unavailable single-concentration measurements are not relabeled as inactive. Full source flags and excluded observations remain available for alternative policies.

The concurrently expanded corpus integration owns cross-source deduplication and final incremental counts. These prepared data do not themselves establish an OOF accuracy improvement.
