## RL0220 — Diagnosing the activity gate on blind chemistry and testing a filtered vector

**2026-10-03 (JST).** The reviewer puts two established results in apparent conflict: 40 of the
49 deployed D6 calls sit below gate `p_active` 0.5 and 21 below 0.3, while RL0219 found 0
positives among 638 labelled rows at primary `log2fc >= -0.25`, yet RL0218 attributes 11 scored
true positives to that payload. Three recorded runs resolve it. Nothing was fitted, no candidate
or prepared submission was created, and the API was not contacted.

**The contradiction dissolves; the premise joining the two facts is false.** `p_active` estimates
`P(log2fc < -1)` while the cross-enzyme rule concerns `log2fc >= -0.25`, and the band between
them is where the labels live: 297 of the 1,497 official D6 rows have `log2fc >= -1` and 22.2% of
them are positive against a 21.6% base rate. Run `20261003T041642823714Z-1ab63580` measures each
link. **The gate is well calibrated on development held folds**: expected calibration error
**.0198** (umap) and **.0243** (scaffold) over ten bins, mean prediction equal to the .2738 base
rate to four decimals, Brier .149 and .142 against .199, AUROC .805 and .825, and the bin
prediction inside the Wilson interval in 9 and 8 of 10 bins; the only systematic deviation is
over-prediction by .025 in the lowest bin. **The blind distribution is shifted up** by +.028 and
+.032 in the mean (KS .056 and .095) and is indistinguishable from the unscreened development
stratum (mean difference +.011 and +.022, AUROC .481 and .510), against clear separation from
screened hits (.350, .343) and non-hits (.646, .682); the 1,770 unscreened rows sit .013 to .024
above the screened ones, AUROC .551 and .559. So the blind extrapolation is interpolation inside
the gate's observed range.

**The decisive measurement is that the gate carries no positive information about the endpoint.**
Among the 1,497 officially labelled rows the positive rate by `p_active` band is
**.231 / .275 / .211 / .183** (umap) and **.230 / .264 / .230 / .181** (scaffold) across
[0,0.2), [0.2,0.3), [0.3,0.5), [0.5,1]; AUROC of `p_active` for TDI is **.4514** and **.4608**,
below one half, with Spearman **−.0694 (p = .0072)** and **−.0559 (p = .0304)**. Composing the
gate's primary-region shares with the measured region rates (.2150 for `< -1`, .2222 for
`[-1,-0.5)` from D6 labels, .0091 for `[-0.5,-0.25)` pooled) gives a blind row below `p_active`
0.2 an **implied positive rate of .033 to .038**, not nothing, because 18% of such rows are still
primary hits. That decomposition reproduces the published .0706 scored prevalence to within a
factor 1.16–1.22 with the relabelled region clean, and overshoots by a factor **1.75** if the
relabelled region is set to the blind base rate, so **of the two offered horns the second is the
one the prevalence identity disfavours and the first is contradicted by the reliability curve.**
Neither is needed. One further measurement matters for the metric: `p_active` separates the
official from the pseudo-negative stratum at AUROC **.805** and **.823**, with 82% of rows above
`p_active` 0.5 being official, so **any metric that scores pseudo-negative labels rewards a gate
filter partly by construction**.

**The filtered vector fails its own pre-declared rule on every threshold.** Run
`20261003T042011147407Z-15430428` rebuilds the deployed recipe's OOF analogue, forms the filters
at tau 0.2/0.3/0.4/0.5 plus the product and square-root product forms, and scores them on the

.23/.42) with a 1,000-resample paired group bootstrap. At 49/750 the best threshold tau = 0.2 is
**+.0201 [−.0054, +.0478]** (umap) and **+.0084 [−.0129, +.0317]** (scaffold) under Metric M and
**−.0090 [−.0310, +.0149]** and **−.0157 [−.0367, +.0059]** under Metric O. tau = 0.3 and 0.4 are
significantly negative under Metric O on umap (−.0365 [−.0728, −.0031] and
−.0525 [−.0984, −.0098]); tau = 0.5 and both multiplier forms are significantly negative on both
families, down to **−.0849 [−.1323, −.0415]**. Under Metric O the unfiltered control has the best
